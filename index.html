👋About

I am Haoyan Zhang, an undergraduate in Electrical Engineering and Automation at Dalian University of Science and Technology. My interests lie in embedded systems, motor-drive control and lightweight computer vision.

My work spans firmware, PCB design and prototype integration, with projects in electrical monitoring, UWB access control and model-guided irrigation. I have published a PMSM control paper and currently study rail-surface defect segmentation and dual-vector PMSM control. More details are in my CV.

I completed my motor-control paper under the guidance of my former course instructor Liang Shen. My research has also benefited from the guidance of Professor Wei Dong, Dean of the School of Transportation and Electrical Engineering.
